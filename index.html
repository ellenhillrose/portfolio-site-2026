Ellen Rose

About

I’m a Senior Experience Designer with eight years of experience across digital products, services, systems, and transformation work, built on nearly a decade in set design, exhibition design, and fine art.

I have an MFA in Products of Design from the School of Visual Arts, and eight years in experience design, built on nearly a decade in set design, exhibition design, and fine art.

Since April 2026 I’ve been part of SYPartners’ in-house AI Incubator, where I design and build AI tools for clients.

My approach combines systems thinking, research, elevated visual craft, and high emotional intelligence. AI is integral in my design process, but I believe that the creative process remains a profoundly human practice. Here’s my point of view: 5 Design Principles for the AI Era

Outside of work I’m a visual artist and old-home renovator. My house and I were featured in an episode of HBO Max’s “Diary of an Old Home.”

I’ll always be a New Yorker at heart, but I now happily call Portland, OR home.
