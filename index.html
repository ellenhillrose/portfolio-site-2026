Ellen Rose

About

I'm a creative and strategic senior designer with an MFA in Products of Design from the School of Visual Arts, and a background in set design and fine art.

My approach combines design-thinking methodologies, elevated visual craft and high emotional intelligence.

I’m currently leading design on client engagements at SYPartners, a premier transformation consultancy. I use the power of design to untangle today’s gnarliest business problems through the lens of experience, product and AI enablement.

I'm a New Yorker who now happily calls Portland, OR home.
